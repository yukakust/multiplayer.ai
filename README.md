# multiplayer.ai

> Can many small intelligences become smarter than one big AI?

We don't know.

Let's find out.

`i + i > AI ?`

multiplayer.ai is an open laboratory.

No promised answer.  
No architecture pretending to be one.  
One small, reproducible experiment at a time.

## i

individual  
intelligence  
information  
a person  
a match

`i` learns locally.  
`i` owns its memory.  
`i` can teach another `i`.

## The lab

```text
doors/         questions people enter through
hunts/         public investigations
journal/       observations they bring
ı/             ideas before evidence
experiments/   tests
i/             results that earned their dot
```

Every path through the lab is simple:

```text
door → hunt → journal → ı → experiment → i → ?
```

Enter through a [door](doors/), or start with the [hypothesis pool](ı/POOL.md).

Each result must say whether it supports, challenges, or leaves the main
question unanswered. Inconclusive and failed experiments belong here too.

## Rules

- Ask one sharp question.
- Use the smallest honest test.
- Compare against a strong, simple baseline.
- Make the result reproducible.
- Publish what failed.
- Let evidence place the dot.

An `i` may live in code, on paper, on a sticker, or on a wall.

Bring a question. Run a test. Leave an `i`.
